Lewis Hamilton is a British Formula 1 driver born in 1985 in Stevenage, England. He drives for Ferrari with the number 44 and is widely regarded as one of the greatest drivers in F1 history. Hamilton won his first world championship with McLaren in 2008 and then dominated the sport with Mercedes, winning six consecutive titles between 2014 and 2020. He holds the record for most wins and pole positions in F1 history. In 2025 he made a high-profile move to Ferrari, fulfilling a lifelong dream of racing for the iconic Italian team.
