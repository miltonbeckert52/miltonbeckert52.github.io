Charles Leclerc is a Monégasque Formula 1 driver born in 1997 in Monte Carlo, Monaco. He drives for Ferrari with the number 16 and has been racing in F1 since 2018. Known for his exceptional qualifying pace and smooth driving style, Leclerc has become one of the sport's biggest stars. He took his first F1 win at the 2019 Belgian Grand Prix and followed it up immediately with another at Monza. In 2025 he finished sixth in the championship, continuing to be a key figure at Ferrari as they rebuild towards a title challenge.
