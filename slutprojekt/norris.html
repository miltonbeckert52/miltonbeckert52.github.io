Lando Norris is a British Formula 1 driver born in 1999 in Bristol, England. He drives for McLaren with the number 4 and has been racing in F1 since 2019. He is known for his natural speed and fun personality. The 2024 season was his breakthrough year with 6 wins and a second place finish in the championship. In 2025 he went one better and became World Champion, winning 7 races and clinching the title by just two points over Max Verstappen at the final race in Abu Dhabi
